Fallback manual method

  1. Tap Copy UPI ID.
  2. Open your UPI app normally.
  3. Choose Pay / Send Money.
  4. Paste the UPI ID and enter the amount.
  5. Verify the recipient name before paying.

If a button does not open the app, that result is useful: it means that the installed app/browser combination is not accepting that app-specific URI.
